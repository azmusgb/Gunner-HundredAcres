Pooh's Honey Hunt - Help Guide

🎮 How to Play

Objective

Help Winnie the Pooh catch as many honey pots as possible while avoiding angry bees!

Controls

• Move Pooh with your mouse or touch
• Use arrow keys for keyboard control
• Press SPACE to pause
• Press ESC to return to menu

🍯 Game Elements

Honey Pots

• Normal pots: +10 points
• Golden pots: +30 points (rare)
• Catch pots to increase your streak!

Bees 🐝

• Regular bees: Lose 1 life on hit
• Angry bees (red): Lose 2 lives on hit
• Bees reset your streak to zero

Power-ups

• Shield: Protects from bees (activates when hit)
• Magnet: Attracts honey pots for 10 seconds
• Slow Time: Slows everything for 8 seconds
• Double Points: 2x points for 12 seconds

🏆 Scoring & Levels

Scoring

• Base points increase with your streak
• Level up every 100 points
• Higher levels = faster falling objects

Difficulty Levels

• Easy: Slow bees, many honey pots
• Medium: Balanced gameplay
• Hard: Fast bees, fewer pots, special challenges

💡 Tips & Tricks

Strategy

• Focus on maintaining your streak for bonus points
• Watch for power-up activations when your power bar is full
• In higher levels, prioritize dodging bees over catching pots
• Use the magnet power-up to catch multiple pots at once

Power Management

• Your power bar fills as you catch honey pots
• When full, you can activate a random power-up
• Click the power bar or press 'P' to activate
• Each power-up has a cooldown period

❓ FAQ

How do I save my progress?

Your high scores are automatically saved in your browser's local storage. No account needed!

Can I play on mobile?

Yes! The game is fully responsive and works on touch devices.

Is there sound?

Sound effects are available. Toggle with the sound button.
