Chapter One

A Special Day in the Hundred Acre Wood

An Invitation to Celebrate

One fine day, in a corner of the Hundred Acre Wood where the sun shines warmly through the trees and the flowers bloom in cheerful colors, something wonderful was happening.

It was the very best sort of celebration—the kind that welcomes a new little friend to the world. A baby boy named Gunnar James was soon to arrive, and everyone wanted to share in the joy.

Pooh, being a Bear of Very Little Brain but a Very Big Heart, knew the most important thing for any celebration was sweetness, laughter, and a little bit of honey.

Chapter Three

Gifts from the Heart

Preparing for Baby Gunnar's Arrival

Pooh sat thoughtfully by his honey pots. "Honey is wonderful," he mused, "but I wonder what else a growing bear—oh, I mean baby—might need?"

Piglet came scurrying up, holding something soft. "Perhaps something cozy for chilly evenings?"

"Something soft and warm, for when the wind howls!"

— Piglet

Soon, all the friends gathered with their ideas. Tigger bounced about toys, Eeyore suggested something useful, and Rabbit began making a very organized list.

"The most important thing," said Pooh wisely, "is that Baby Gunnar knows he is surrounded by love."

— Pooh Bear
